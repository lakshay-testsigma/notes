<Analogy>
Vibe coding is riding shotgun while the AI drives blindfolded; agentic coding
puts you in the pilot's seat with the AI as your skilled co-pilot.
</Analogy>

<TLDR>
- **Claude Code** = an agentic coding tool from Anthropic — a partner that understands your whole codebase, not autocomplete.
- **Vibe coding** (prompt → generate → fix-loop) is fine for MVPs, fatal when real money is on the line.
- **Agentic coding** keeps you technical: you instruct, review, and decide — that's what this course teaches, not vibes.
</TLDR>

## Where it works vs where it fails

| Works well (low stakes) | Fails (high stakes) |
| --- | --- |
| MVPs | Scalable systems (Google-level) |
| Exploring / prototyping ideas | Critical infrastructure software |
| Hackathon projects | Banking & insurance software |
| Any low-stakes project | Anything with a lot of money on the line |
